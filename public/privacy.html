Privacy Policy

SRT loves VLC (“the app”) is a macOS menu-bar helper published by Bo Kelleher.

What we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Recent stream URLs and settings (VLC path, latency, launch-at-login preferences) stay on your Mac in standard app preferences.

Network

The app does not contact our servers. It only hands srt:// destinations to VLC on your Mac. Network traffic for playback is handled by VLC under your control.

Third parties

The app is not affiliated with VideoLAN. VLC is a separate product with its own privacy practices.
