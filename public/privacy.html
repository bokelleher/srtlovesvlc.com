Privacy Policy

SRT loves VLC (“the app”) is a macOS menu-bar and Windows tray helper published by Bo Kelleher.

01

What we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Recent stream URLs and settings (VLC path, latency, launch-at-login preferences) stay on your computer in standard app preferences. If a stream link carries credentials such as a passphrase, they are kept locally with that recent entry and nowhere else.

02

Network

The app does not contact our servers. It only hands srt:// destinations to VLC on your computer. Network traffic for playback is handled by VLC under your control.

03

Purchases

Direct purchases are processed by Stripe. Stripe handles your payment details under its own privacy policy; we receive a receipt and the private download link we issue to you, nothing more.

04

Third parties

The app is not affiliated with VideoLAN. VLC is a separate product with its own privacy practices.

05

Contact

Questions: bo@bokelleher.com · srtlovesvlc.com
